## 1. The Platform

Olympusverse is a creator marketplace and digital ecosystem. It includes:

- **Omega Store** — a retail storefront selling apparel, drinkware, wall art, accessories, and other print-on-demand products.
- **Custom Product Designer** — a tool that lets you upload your own graphics, place them on products, set your price, and sell them.
- **Creator Pages** — personal storefronts where your custom creations live for sale.
- **Wallet System** — account-tied balances where earnings accumulate.

Products are fulfilled by third-party print-on-demand suppliers (currently Printful; additional suppliers may be added).

## 2. Creator Terms

### 2.1 Your Designs
When you upload a graphic to the Custom Product Designer:
- You confirm you own the rights to the design or have permission to use it.
- You grant the Platform a license to reproduce your design on products for fulfillment.
- You retain ownership of your original artwork.

### 2.2 Pricing and Markup
Before your creation goes live:
- You set your markup — the amount you earn per sale above the base production cost.
- Base cost + your markup = the retail price the customer pays.
- Your markup is your income. Choose it carefully; it cannot be changed after the product goes live without removing and relisting.

### 2.3 Creator Pages
- Every creator gets a personal page showcasing all their custom products.
- Your page is your storefront within the store. Share it, promote it — your sales are your earnings.

### 2.4 Content Rules
- **Original work only.** Do not upload copyrighted material you don't own, trademarks you don't control, or anyone else's art.
- **No duplicates.** The same graphic may appear on different product types, but never twice on the same product type. Mirrored or flipped versions count as duplicates.
- **Prohibited content:** hate speech, explicit sexual content, graphic violence, content promoting illegal activity, or anything that violates applicable law.
- We reserve the right to remove any product that violates these rules, with or without notice.

## 4. Earnings, Wallets, and Accumulation Mode

### 4.1 How Creator Earnings Work
- When someone buys your custom product, your markup goes into your Platform wallet.
- Every transaction is recorded with a full timestamped history you can review.

### 4.2 Accumulation Mode (Current Status)
The Platform is currently operating in **accumulation mode**:
- All earnings — yours, ours, everyone's — accumulate in wallet balances.
- Withdrawals are not yet available.
- Funds will become withdrawable when regulatory compliance is complete (see Section 5).

This is temporary. We are building toward full compliance, not around it.

### 4.3 Wallet Terms
- Your wallet is tied to your Platform account. It is not a bank account.
- Balances do not earn interest.
- We do not lend, invest, or otherwise use your wallet balance.
- Minimum withdrawal thresholds and processing times will be published when withdrawals go live.

## 5. Regulatory Compliance

We take compliance seriously. The Platform is working toward:

- **FINTRAC registration** (Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada) — for money-services obligations.
- **Bank of Canada Retail Payments Activities Act (RPAA) registration** — required before Interac/e-transfer payouts can operate.
- **Interac compliance** — for direct bank transfers to Canadian users.

Until these are complete:
- All funds remain in accumulation mode (Section 4.2).
- No withdrawals, payouts, or transfers out of the Platform.
- We will announce when each compliance gate clears.

We are building this to meet regulatory standards, not to evade them. If you have questions about our compliance status, contact us.
