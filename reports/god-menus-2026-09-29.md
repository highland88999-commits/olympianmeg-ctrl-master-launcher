# God navigation menus + orbit-ring home scene — 2026-09-29

## What shipped

The launcher home now has **10 god-named navigation menus** (the navigation
layer) and a rebuilt 3D home scene where **all 128 satellites ride 10 orbital
bands around the constellation center, over the Earth** — one band per god, so
the menu groupings are visible in 3D. The camera **spawns in the middle** of the
bands looking outward; the user's **TRON bike is parked just outside the bands,
dead ahead**, with a **Spawn to Bike** button (bottom-right) that flies the
camera into the rider's seat.

## 3D home scene (Merlin's 4 requirements)

1. **Satellites orbit Earth** — `components/GodOrbitRings.jsx`: 10 tilted
   orbital bands (radii 46–65.8, clear of the constellation towers at r≈40)
   around the constellation center `(0, 35, -80)`, hovering over the Earth at
   `(0, -60, -120)`. Each band carries its god's satellites as glowing nodes
   (emissive god-accent, fog off so they stay crisp) with a dim band ring and
   a floating god-name label. Bands drift slowly on one shared clock.
2. **Spawn in the middle** — the camera now starts at the constellation
   center `(0, 35, -80)` and slowly pans during the intro (any input skips to
   free-roam), so the user is ringed by satellites looking outward. Previously
   it spawned far outside at `(0, 75, 20)`.
3. **TRON bike waiting** — `components/BikeRig.jsx` reuses the existing
   `WispBike` model from `components/nova/TronBikes.jsx` (same bike family as
   nova/argo/myriad — no duplicate model). Parked at `(0, 35, ≈-152)`, just
   outside the outermost band on the −Z view axis, nose toward the user, with
   a pulsing teal landing beacon and a "YOUR RIDE" label. First thing seen
   ahead on load.
4. **Spawn to Bike button** — fixed bottom-right (`bottom-5 right-5`,
   `ui-element` so it never fights the joystick). Tapping flies the camera
   into the rider's seat (`to_bike` → `on_bike` states in the
   SequenceController); the mounted camera rides the bike's hover bob facing
   the constellation, drag-to-look works. Tapping again dismounts back to
   free-roam. (Visible rider model = the queued mounted-rider work, later.)

**Shared orbit math** — `lib/god-orbits.js` is pure math (no three.js): band
layout, the shared `orbitTime()` clock, and `orbitPositionFor(satKey, t)`,
which mirrors the rendered nested-group transform exactly (verified against
real three.js matrices: max error 2.9e-14). Both the scene and
`launchPocketUniverse` use it, so a tapped orbit node and a god-menu button
always drop onto the same live position.

**`launchPocketUniverse` stays the single entry point.** Signature is now
`(route, satKey, fallbackPos, directPos)`: orbit-node taps and god-menu
buttons resolve the live orbit position; tower ad panels pass their explicit
position; constellation `POS_MAP` remains the fallback. The 2D panel
(`GodMenuPanel`) and the 3D nodes both call only this — no direct routing
anywhere.

Also fixed: a `useMemo` that had been placed inside conditional JSX (hook-order
violation) now lives at the top of `NexusHub`.
