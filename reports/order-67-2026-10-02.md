# ORDER 67 — build report (2026-10-02)

Merlin green-lit at ~01:19 MDT ("You go ahead!!!"). Holographic P2P calling pocket universe at `/order-67`, in the HERMES god menu ("Transit, Comms & Shopping").

## What shipped

**The Throne** (`app/order-67/throne-room.js`) — Three.js holochamber: dark circular chamber (procedural floor rings + wall panels, canvas textures, zero downloads), central projection dais with a glowing cyan trim ring, holoprojector with lens, volumetric additive light cone, 130 drifting dust motes, restrained localized lighting (ambient + one cyan point light; no bloom pass — phone-perf). Gentle camera drift + rotate-only orbit controls (no pan/zoom). `prefers-reduced-motion` disables drift, dust, and flicker. Pixel ratio capped at 1.5.

**The hologram** — custom ShaderMaterial on a plane standing on the dais, sampling the peer's video via VideoTexture: cyan/blue monochrome from luminance, scanlines, layered-sine flicker, subtle vertical wobble, additive translucency, and a materialize wipe that rises from the bottom with a bright leading edge. Local self-view stays a small normal corner preview (mirrored).

**"Execute Order 67"** (`app/order-67/page.jsx`) — the big button hosts a call: creates an OASIS-style 6-char room, shows the shareable code, does non-trickle WebRTC offer/answer through the room row. Join flow takes a code. Solo mode ("Summon test hologram") loops your own camera through the hologram shader — works with no peer, no sign-in.

**Signaling** (`app/order-67/signaling.js` + `lib/oasis/rooms.js`) — reuses the OASIS room pattern exactly: `oasis_rooms` rows, ~2s DB polling via `subscribeToRoom`, zero Realtime. SDP offer/answer ride in `full_state` as `{ v: 1, offer, answer }`; each side writes once (non-trickle ICE), so the host's offer write and the guest's read-modify-write answer can't clobber each other. No new backend invented.

**Realm backdrops** (Merlin's refinement) — bottom control panel with a backdrop picker: VOID + 10 god-realm chips (horizontal scroll, 44px targets). Loads `/images/order-67/backdrops/backdrop-<realm>.webp` (fallback `.jpg`) onto a large curved plane behind the dais, dimmed 50% so the hologram stays the focus. Missing files fall back to the procedural void silently — v1 works fully with zero backdrop files. Selection persists in localStorage.

**Privacy honesty** — the UI states it plainly: "Peer-to-peer: video and audio travel directly between the two phones. The signal server only sees room codes — never your media." STUN is documented in `lib/order-67.js` (public STUN only helps the phones find each other; self-hosted TURN is the future upgrade).
