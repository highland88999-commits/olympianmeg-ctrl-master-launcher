# EREBUS — 140 Void Universes (2026-09-29)

Merlin's directive: a pure escape space — no programs, no modules inside, just a
void to get lost in for a while. One portal, the tron bike, 140 seeded
universes, 2 hidden gold bars in each.

## What was built

**`lib/erebus-universes.js`** (new) — pure headless logic, no DOM/THREE/network:
- 140 deterministic universes (`generateUniverses`, seed 20260930), each with a
  dark-cosmic name, palette, star density, 6–9 planets, 2–3 asteroid fields.
- `sceneFor(universe)`: planets (+0–3 orbiting moons each), 40–69 rocks per
  asteroid field, 2–3 nebula accents, ONE fixed portal ring, exactly 2 gold
  bars — bar 0 in the open (100–260 units from spawn, clear of planets), bar 1
  tucked (behind a planet, near a moon, or at a field edge; always within 340
  of spawn; always near *something* — pinned by tests).
- `obstaclesFor` / `resolveCollision`: bounce, never die.
- Progress + append-only local gold ledger, localStorage-only, per-scope
  (`device:<rand>` or `user:<supabase-id>` when signed in). 1,000 liquid gold
  per bar (gold economy v2: 1,000 liquid = 1 bar). Idempotent collect/credit.
- No telemetry, no network, no secrets (asserted by tests).

**`app/erebus/page.jsx`** (new, route `/erebus`):
- Atlas view: searchable 140-universe grid (name/number search, all/unvisited/
  bars-left filters), tron bike garage (Wisp/Comet/Atlas), visited + bars +
  liquid-gold totals.
- Explorer view: third-person TRON bike flight on the NOVA flight model
  (`lib/nova-flight.js` math, `TronBikes` garage, `TronRider`), chase cam,
  touch joystick (steer + thrust) + keyboard (WASD/arrows, R/F pitch, Shift
  boost, Space brake, P portal), autopilot tour toggle (guides to remaining
  bars then the portal; any user input disengages), reduced-motion support.
- Gold bars render with a tall faint beacon beam + pulsing glint (visible from
  far); ride close to collect; toast confirms; ledger credits.
- The ONE portal: a fixed ring in-world ("Portal — fly in to jump") + one
  centered HUD button; both open the jump sheet (searchable universe list).
- Minimal HUD: universe name/index, bars ◆ n/2, portal button. Safe-area
  clearance throughout; no pop-ups.

**`lib/god-menus.js`** — registered `'erebus'` in the god-menu satellite list.

**`tests/erebus-universes.test.js`** (new) — 113 assertions: identity,
determinism, scene contents, bar difficulty pins, portal placement, collision,
progress/ledger idempotency, and source-level checks (no telemetry/network/
secrets, bike/flight/gold/portal/autopilot present, erebus registered).

## Solid geometry — Merlin's standing rule (confirmed 2026-09-29)

In EREBUS, ALL geometry is solid: planets, orbiting moons, and every asteroid
rock are collision obstacles. The bike bounces/deflects (speed cut to 25% +
contact-normal push-out + tap feedback) — never dies, never clips through.
Collision is **substepped** (max 3 units per substep) so fast flight can't
tunnel through small rocks between frames. The ONLY pass-through is the portal
ring, by design (flying in opens the jump sheet). Non-solid by nature and
intentionally so: nebula puffs (volumetric light, not bodies), star points
(unreachable), and the gold-bar beacon beam (a light shaft; the bar itself is
collected on approach, before any visual contact is possible).

Proven, not just asserted: the test suite marches a bike-sized sphere along
40 rays per universe across **all 140 universes**, substepped exactly like the
page, and asserts the resting position never ends up inside any obstacle —
worst measured penetration: 0.
