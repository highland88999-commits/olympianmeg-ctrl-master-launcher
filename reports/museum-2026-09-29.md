# MUSEUM — the gallery of Merlin's creative output — 2026-09-29

## What it is
New pocket-universe module **MUSEUM** (`/museum`), from the Greek *Mouseion* —
a public, timeless gallery and portfolio of Merlin's creative output, built
with the weight of what Miles inherits: refined, minimal, clean.

Three wings:
- **Gallery** — 107 curated Tier-A artworks in 11 series, searchable and
  series-filtered, with a full lightbox (keyboard nav, kept-piece hearts).
- **Films** — 4 concept films from the archive (AIR GENESIS air-car Veo clip,
  APIS // NEXUS logo reveal, Medusa character study, OLYMPUS neon-city drive).
- **Concepts** — 25 curated highlights from the 131-concept catalog, each with
  an honest plain-English status label (Shipped / Prototype / Idea / Spec /
  Not approved). Titans of Tartarus appears once, labeled "Not approved —
  historical only, never built, never presented as planned."

## Series (11)
Valkyrie · Gold Robot · Neon · Poster Studies · Branding · Fortress of
Solitude · Gems · Starblood · InnoDigym · Key Art · Textures

## Rules honored
- No Supabase changes; "Kept" hearts persist in localStorage only.
- No telemetry, no external requests, no hotlinks, no remote fonts.
- Curated, not a dump — every piece has a title; key pieces have captions.
- Lighting never obscures artwork (flat 2D gallery, no 3D effects).
- Scrolls correctly; clears the radio/Safari bar with safe-area padding.
- Plain-English, honest labels throughout.
