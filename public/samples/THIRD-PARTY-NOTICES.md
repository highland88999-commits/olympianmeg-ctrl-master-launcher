## Salamander Grand Piano V3 — `piano/`

- Source: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm
  (https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3)
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)**

## FluidR3 General MIDI SoundFont — `trumpet/`, `violin/`, `guitar/`, `electric-guitar/`

- Source: FluidR3 GM SoundFont by Frank Wen and contributors
  (https://github.com/urish/cinto/raw/master/media/FluidR3%20GM.sf2 —
  mirror of the upstream FluidR3 release)
- License: **MIT License** (upstream: "I hereby release Fluid under the MIT

- What we ship: short derived multisamples rendered from GM presets
